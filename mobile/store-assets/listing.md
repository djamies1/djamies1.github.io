## Full Description (4000 chars max, works for both stores)

**Know exactly what to grow — every month.**

Plant Zone Finder is your personal gardening companion. Find your USDA hardiness zone or climate zone instantly, get a month-by-month planting guide tailored to your location, and track every crop from seed to harvest.

**🗺️ Find Your Zone**
Tap any location on the map or use GPS to instantly identify your growing zone. Covers all USDA hardiness zones across the United States, Canadian hardiness zones, Australian climate zones, UK RHS zones, and New Zealand regions.

**📅 Monthly Planting Calendar**
Know exactly what to start indoors, direct sow, transplant, and harvest — every single month. Guides for 69 crops including vegetables, herbs, and fruit, personalised to your zone.

**🌿 My Garden Tracker**
Save the crops you're growing, log your planting dates, and let the app count down the days to harvest. Get alerted when crops are ready to pick.

**🐛 Pest & Problem Log**
Record pest sightings and disease problems by crop. Mark issues as resolved and build a history of what affected your garden — invaluable for season planning.

**📡 Works Fully Offline**
Installs to your home screen like a native app. All planting data, your garden, and your notes are stored on-device. No internet connection required after first load.

**🌤️ Weather & Frost Alerts**
Live 7-day weather strip with a Best Planting Day forecast. Frost date countdown tells you exactly when to transplant tender seedlings outdoors.

**🌾 Browse 69 Crops**
Full growing profiles: days to maturity, companion plants, spacing, light requirements, and seasonal highlights — all searchable and filterable.

**📖 Garden Journal**
Log notes, observations, and milestones for each growing season. Look back at previous seasons in the Garden Archive.

**Completely free. No account required. No ads.**

## App Store Review Notes (for reviewers)
This is a Progressive Web App (PWA) wrapped with Capacitor. No account or login is required. All data is stored locally on-device. The app uses:
- Device location (optional, for zone detection only)
- Local notifications (optional, for frost/harvest reminders)
No other sensitive permissions are requested.
