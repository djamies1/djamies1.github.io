About

Who I Am

I'm a Business Intelligence Engineer with over a decade of experience translating raw data into tools that actually move the needle. My career spans accounting roots at EY, financial analytics at Inspired LED and Falck USA, enterprise BI leadership at PAC Worldwide, and now building large-scale data products at Amazon.

I specialise in the full data stack — architecting warehouses, writing ETL pipelines in SQL and Python, and crafting dashboards that 600+ users rely on daily. I thrive at the intersection of technical depth and business impact, partnering with HR, Finance, and executive stakeholders to deliver analytics that drive real decisions.

Most recently, I've been embedded in Amazon Leo (formerly Project Kuiper), building financial analytics products as the satellite internet service heads toward general availability. I leverage AWS Bedrock and AI-assisted development to accelerate delivery — a genuinely exciting challenge at the frontier of space tech and AI-powered analytics.
